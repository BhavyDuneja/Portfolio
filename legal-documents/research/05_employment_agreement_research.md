# Research File 05 — Employment Agreement

**Document purpose:** The master agreement governing the employment relationship between Sutranet and an individual employee. The Employee NDA (file 02) and IP Assignment Deed (file 03) sit alongside or are incorporated by reference. The Employment Agreement is the document that any employment-tribunal or labour-court will look at first.

---

## 1. Overview & purpose

In India, the Employment Agreement (EA) does several jobs simultaneously:

1. **Records the offer**: title, joining date, location, compensation.
2. **Crystallises the statutory framework**: working hours, leave, PF/ESI/gratuity entitlements consistent with applicable labour laws.
3. **Creates contractual restraints** that complement statutory protections — confidentiality, IP, non-solicit, notice period, garden leave.
4. **Sets disciplinary and termination procedure**, consistent with the principles of natural justice.
5. **Prescribes conduct standards**: code of conduct, POSH, anti-bribery, DPDP, IT/acceptable use.
6. **Serves as the evidentiary anchor** in any disputed termination, PF/ESI assessment, or IT-Act prosecution.

A poorly drafted EA is the single largest source of HR and labour-law risk for Indian SMEs. The drafter must navigate **central laws**, **state-specific S&E Acts**, **the four labour codes (when fully notified)**, and **POSH Act 2013**.

## 2. Governing law & statutory basis

| Statute / Instrument | Relevance |
|----------------------|-----------|
| **Indian Contract Act, 1872** | Foundational. s.10, 23, 27, 28, 73, 74. |
| **Specific Relief Act, 1963** | s.14(c) — contract of personal service is *not* specifically enforceable, so termination cannot be undone but damages and reinstatement (under labour laws) are available. |
| **Shops & Establishments Act (state-specific)** | Karnataka S&CE Act 1961, Maharashtra S&E Act 2017, Delhi S&E Act 1954, Tamil Nadu S&E Act 1947, etc. Governs hours, weekly off, leave, holidays, termination notice. **EA cannot give less than statutory minima.** Registration of establishment under the relevant Act is mandatory once headcount thresholds are crossed (varies by state; Karnataka: any establishment). |
| **Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946** | Applies to industrial establishments employing ≥ 100 workmen (50 in some states). Standing Orders, once certified, override individual EAs for "workmen". Most Sutranet engineering staff will not be workmen, but junior support / data-ops staff might be. |
| **Industrial Disputes Act, 1947** | Applies to "workmen" — termination procedure under s.25F (retrenchment), s.25N (in establishments ≥ 100 workmen, prior government permission), s.2A (individual disputes). EA cannot waive these. |
| **Code on Wages, 2019** | Replaces Payment of Wages Act, Minimum Wages Act, Payment of Bonus Act, Equal Remuneration Act. Notified but not fully operationalised — drafter should refer generically to "applicable wages legislation". |
| **Industrial Relations Code, 2020** | Replaces ID Act, IE(SO) Act, Trade Unions Act. Same notification status. |
| **Code on Social Security, 2020** | Replaces EPF Act 1952, ESI Act 1948, Gratuity Act 1972, Maternity Benefit Act 1961. |
| **Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020** | Replaces Factories Act, S&E Acts, Contract Labour Act, Inter-State Migrant Workmen Act. |
| **Employees' Provident Funds & Misc. Provisions Act, 1952 (until OSH Code fully replaces)** | Mandatory for establishments with ≥ 20 employees; 12% employer + 12% employee contribution on basic + DA, capped at ₹15,000 for statutory minimum (often paid on full basic for retention). |
| **Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948** | Applies in notified areas to employees earning ≤ ₹21,000/month. |
| **Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972** | Mandatory after 5 years' continuous service; 15 days' wages × completed years. |
| **Maternity Benefit Act, 1961** | 26 weeks' paid leave for first two children, 12 weeks for third+; crèche facility for ≥ 50 employees. |
| **POSH Act 2013** (Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act) | Mandatory Internal Committee (IC) for ≥ 10 employees; policy publication; annual report. |
| **Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016** | Reasonable accommodation; non-discrimination. |
| **Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Act, 2019** | Non-discrimination. |
| **Equal Remuneration Act, 1976 (now Code on Wages)** | Equal pay for equal work irrespective of gender. |
| **Apprentices Act, 1961** | If engaging apprentices. |
| **Information Technology Act, 2000** | s.43, 43A, 65, 66, 72A — employee handling of digital data and source code. |
| **DPDP Act, 2023** | Employee personal data is processed by Sutranet as data fiduciary; consent / notice required (interaction with employment-purpose lawful basis still under regulatory clarification). |
| **Income-tax Act, 1961** | TDS on salary u/s 192; perquisite valuation; ESOP taxation u/s 17(2)(vi). |
| **Indian Stamp Act, 1899 + state schedules** | EA is "agreement not otherwise provided for". |
| **Companies Act, 2013** | s.197 (managerial remuneration for KMP); s.149/150 (ID); Schedule V (disqualifications). |

### Case-law touchstones

- *Niranjan Shankar Golikari v. Century Spinning*, AIR 1967 SC 1098 — restraints during employment OK; post-employment non-compete void.
- *Superintendence Co. v. Krishan Murgai*, AIR 1980 SC 1717 — post-termination non-compete struck down.
- *Percept D'Mark v. Zaheer Khan*, (2006) 4 SCC 227 — right of first refusal as restraint.
- *Gujarat Bottling v. Coca Cola*, (1995) 5 SCC 545 — restraints during currency of contract enforceable; commercial context.
- *Sicpa India v. Manas Pratim Deb*, (2011) Delhi HC — training-bond reduced to actual cost.
- *VFS Global v. Suprit Roy*, (2008) Bombay HC — garden leave that operated post-termination struck down.
- *Pepsi Foods v. Bharat Coca-Cola Holdings*, 81 (1999) DLT 122 — non-deal disguised as non-solicit struck down.
- *Desiccant Rotors v. Bappaditya Sarkar*, 2009 (110) DRJ 691 — 12-month non-solicit upheld.
- *Vishaka v. State of Rajasthan*, AIR 1997 SC 3011 — POSH guidelines, codified by 2013 Act.

## 3. Mandatory & recommended clauses

### 3.1 Mandatory

1. **Parties** — Company (Sutranet, with CIN, registered office) and Employee (full name, address, PAN/Aadhaar reference).
2. **Position and reporting** — designation, department, reporting manager, location, work-from-home/hybrid arrangement.
3. **Joining date and probation** — typical probation 3–6 months; confirmation in writing; extension permissible once.
4. **Compensation** — gross / CTC; basic + HRA + special allowance + employer PF + gratuity; variable / bonus structure; ESOP reference (if any) under separate ESOP Plan.
5. **Statutory deductions** — PF, ESI (if applicable), professional tax, TDS.
6. **Working hours & leave** — consistent with applicable S&E Act; weekly off; gazetted holidays; earned leave / sick leave / casual leave (typical: 18 EL + 12 CL/SL); maternity / paternity leave; bereavement.
7. **Notice period** — typically 60–90 days for managerial / IT staff, 30 days for junior; mutual; right to pay/buy out in lieu (mutual).
8. **Termination for cause** — gross misconduct, fraud, criminal conviction, material breach of policies; with opportunity to be heard (principles of natural justice).
9. **Confidentiality** — incorporated by reference to Employee NDA (file 02) — *or* full clause set inline if no separate NDA.
10. **IP assignment** — incorporated by reference to IP Assignment Deed (file 03); failing which, full present-tense assignment inline + s.19 Copyright Act parameters + moral rights waiver.
11. **Code of Conduct, POSH Policy, Anti-Bribery Policy, DPDP Policy, IT/Acceptable Use Policy** — reference and acknowledgement; subject to amendment.
12. **Background verification consent** — DPDP-compliant.
13. **Garden leave option** during notice period — paid, no work.
14. **Non-solicit (clients and personnel)** — 12 months post-termination.
15. **Return of property** at exit.
16. **Set-off** of any amounts due against final settlement.
17. **Governing law and jurisdiction.**

### 3.2 Recommended

- **Probation extension and confirmation** procedure.
- **Performance management** reference (PIP, appraisal cycle, goals).
- **Conflict of interest** disclosure obligation.
- **Outside engagements / moonlighting** — prior written consent; no conflict; no use of Company resources.
- **Travel and reimbursement** policy reference.
- **Relocation** — clawback if employee resigns within 12 months of relocation paid for by Company.
- **Training-bond** — actual documented cost, pro-rated by service length.
- **ESOP / RSU** — separate plan referenced; vesting and good-leaver / bad-leaver provisions.
- **Whistleblower** policy reference.
- **POSH** specific reference and acknowledgement.
- **Compliance with Company policies** — generic catch-all subject to "reasonable amendments on prior notice".
- **Survival clauses** for confidentiality, IP, non-solicit, return of property, indemnity.

## 4. Drafting notes, pitfalls, enforceability tips

1. **Workman classification matters.** The EA cannot waive ID Act protections for employees who qualify as "workmen" under s.2(s). Use a generic "subject to applicable labour laws" caveat, and have HR maintain a register of which roles are likely "workman".

2. **State S&E Act minima are floors.** EA can be more generous (longer leave, more notice) but never less. Karnataka, for example, mandates 18 days' annual leave; Maharashtra mandates 21 if employed > 240 days in a year.

3. **Notice period buy-out.** "Pay in lieu of notice" should be **mutual** and the calculation basis should be clear (basic only? gross? CTC?). Many disputes arise from ambiguous clauses. Default: pay in lieu equals **basic + DA** — but state EA expressly to avoid argument.

4. **Garden leave is enforceable during notice** but not post-termination (see *VFS Global v. Suprit Roy*). Draft as an option exercisable by Company *during* the notice period only.

5. **Notice period reduction for early exit.** Some companies "release early at request" but withhold experience certificate / relieving letter. Doing so is risky — a recent line of cases (Karnataka HC, Bombay HC) treats withholding of experience letter as tortious interference. Issue documents and pursue contractual recovery instead.

6. **Probation termination.** During probation, termination *with notice* (typically 30 days or 1 month's pay in lieu) and *without cause* is permissible. Termination *without* notice for misconduct still requires opportunity to be heard.

7. **Non-compete is void post-employment** under s.27 ICA (settled). Drop entirely. Limit to during-employment (which is implicit in any case).

8. **Non-solicit must be reasonable.** 12 months, limited to actively-solicit-not-hire, and limited to clients/employees personally dealt with in 12 months preceding exit.

9. **Training-bond.** Must equal documented cost; not penal lump sum. *Sicpa India v. Manas Pratim Deb* is the leading authority. Add a pro-rata reduction based on service rendered.

10. **ESOP good-leaver / bad-leaver.** Cliff and vesting per the Plan. EA should reference the Plan and not duplicate.

11. **POSH Act compliance.** Mandatory IC; written policy; annual report (filed with District Officer); awareness training. EA should require compliance with POSH policy and acknowledge IC jurisdiction.

12. **Sexual orientation, gender identity, disability** — non-discrimination per Constitution Article 14, 15, 16; RPwD Act; *NALSA v. UoI*, (2014) 5 SCC 438; *Navtej Singh Johar v. UoI*, (2018) 10 SCC 1.

13. **WFH / Hybrid.** New OSH Code allows. EA should clarify location flexibility, and address OSH obligations (ergonomics statement) and reimbursement of home-office expense if any.

14. **DPDP-compliant employee data handling.** Sutranet must give a privacy notice to the employee at hiring; obtain consent for non-employment-purpose processing (e.g., publishing photo on website); maintain retention schedule; allow rights exercise (access, correction, erasure subject to legal/regulatory retention).

15. **Background verification.** DPDP-compliant consent; only proportionate checks (criminal, education, prior employment, address, credit if role-relevant); third-party verifier under contract.

16. **Clawback of joining bonus / sign-on bonus.** Enforceable if pro-rated and reasonable. Lump-sum recovery of full bonus on exit within 12 months is borderline; pro-rata recovery is safe.

17. **PF on full basic vs ₹15,000 cap.** *Regional PF Commissioner v. Vivekananda Vidyamandir*, (2019) — basic includes special allowance if it is paid universally and not contingent on performance — significant cost implication. Some employers contribute on the statutory cap (₹15,000); others on full basic. EA should specify which approach is taken.

18. **Gratuity.** Statutory after 5 years; some employers offer earlier vesting as an HR perk; either is permissible (not less than statutory).

19. **Maternity Benefit Act.** 26 weeks for first two children. Cannot be reduced. Crèche for ≥ 50 employees. Adoption/commissioning mother also covered.

20. **Indemnity by employee.** Limit to losses arising from gross negligence, wilful misconduct, breach of NDA/IP Assignment, or fraud. Do not draft an open-ended indemnity — courts strike as unconscionable.

21. **Severability and saving.** If any clause is held void (especially non-compete), remainder survives. Critical — protect the rest of the EA.

22. **Language and acknowledgement.** EA must be in a language the employee understands (the Industrial Disputes Act and S&E Acts implicitly require this for "workmen"). For Sutranet's mostly English-using technical staff, English is fine; if hiring blue-collar/junior staff, provide a vernacular translation.

23. **No-display of contract terms in offer letter.** Common practice in India: a short Offer Letter + a longer EA signed on Day 1. Ensure consistency between the two — discrepancies cause disputes (e.g., offer letter says 60 days' notice, EA says 90 days).

## 7. Stamp duty & execution requirements

- **Stamp duty:** "Agreement not otherwise provided for" — Karnataka ₹200, Maharashtra ₹100–500, Delhi ₹50–100, Tamil Nadu ₹100. Use SHCIL e-stamp or franking. Always stamp.
- **Registration:** Not required.
- **Execution:** Wet-ink with two witnesses (recommended, not strictly mandatory) or Aadhaar e-Sign with audit trail. Counterparts; one copy to employee, one to HR file, one to Company Secretary.
- **Joining-day kit:** Offer Letter (already issued) → Employment Agreement → Employee NDA → IP Assignment Deed → Acknowledgements of Code of Conduct, POSH Policy, IT/Acceptable Use Policy, DPDP Privacy Notice, Background Verification Consent. All signed Day 1.
