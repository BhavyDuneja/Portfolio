## Risk Analysis

### Market Solutions Risks
1. **Vendor Lock-in**: Difficult and expensive to switch
2. **License Increases**: Annual price increases (5-15%)
3. **Feature Limitations**: May not meet specific needs
4. **Integration Costs**: Expensive to connect systems
5. **Data Control**: Limited access to your own data
6. **Customization Limits**: Restricted modification options

### Custom Solution Risks
1. **Development Risk**: Potential delays or scope changes
2. **Technical Debt**: Need for ongoing maintenance
3. **Scalability**: May require architecture updates
4. **Support**: Dependency on development team

### Risk Mitigation Strategies
1. **Phased Development**: Reduces risk through iterative delivery
2. **Agile Methodology**: Allows for adjustments and feedback
3. **Documentation**: Comprehensive technical documentation
4. **Code Ownership**: Full source code provided
5. **Support Contract**: Ongoing maintenance and support available
