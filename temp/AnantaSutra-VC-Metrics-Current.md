## 1. Revenue terms

### Revenue ✅
**Formula:** money earned for services delivered.
**Yours: ₹2,37,490** over ~3.1 months. July alone: ₹1,17,000.

### GMV vs Net Revenue ⚠️ — with an honest catch about YOUR books
**Formula:** GMV = all money flowing through you; Net revenue = the part that's yours.
**Your current reality:** the books show clients paying AnantaSutra in full and AnantaSutra paying the team — classic full-service flow. So **today, GMV = Net Revenue = ₹2,37,490** and there is no pass-through in the books yet. The "client pays the expert's salary + our fee" structure is your go-forward model, not yet your recorded history. **Say exactly this to a VC** — pretending the platform split already exists would be caught in diligence. The metric activates with your first true salary-pass-through placement.

### Take rate ❌ (not yet applicable)
**Formula:** Net revenue ÷ GMV.
**Yours: N/A until the first pass-through placement.** When it starts, target the 10–15% band — that's the "Deel for embedded experts" headline number.

### MRR ✅⚠️
**Formula:** contracted, repeating monthly revenue only.
**Yours as of Aug: ≈ ₹70,000** (Awish ₹30K expected cadence + Savetime ₹40K due Aug 30). ⚠️ Assumption: both continue at current agreed levels — note your own tracker expected ₹45K from Awish in Aug and received ₹30K; resolve that gap before quoting ₹85K.
**Pipeline MRR:** +₹28K when Gaba Jewellers starts → ₹98K.

### ARR ✅
**Formula:** MRR × 12.
**Yours: ≈ ₹8.4L** (on ₹70K MRR).

### Run-rate ✅

**Yours: ₹14.04L** (July ₹1.17L × 12). Quote as "July run-rate," never as ARR.

## 2. Profitability terms

### COGS ⚠️
**Formula:** costs that scale directly with delivering client work.
**Yours: ≈ ₹64,000–₹1,01,500 depending on classification.** The spread: Abhishek/Saima/Deepali/Amandeep/Nazia/Mohit + designers ≈ ₹64K are clearly client-delivery; **Lakshay (₹37.5K total) is the swing** — if his engineering was client work he's COGS, if internal/product he's OpEx. ❌ **Action: tag every payout "client-delivery" vs "internal" in the expense sheet from this month.** This one tag decides your gross margin.

### Gross Margin ⚠️
**Formula:** (Revenue − COGS) ÷ Revenue.
**Yours: ~57% (if Lakshay = COGS) to ~73% (if internal).** Either way healthy — services norm is 30–50%. Quote "~60% blended" until the tagging is done.

### EBITDA ✅
**Formula:** revenue − all operating costs (at your size, ≈ net profit).
**Yours: ₹1,01,698 over the period → ~42.8% EBITDA margin.** This is your single most impressive number — profitable from month one. Say it early in every pitch.

### Burn rate & Runway ✅
**Formula:** net cash lost per month; runway = cash ÷ burn.
**Yours: burn is NEGATIVE (≈ +₹33K/month average, ≈ +₹77K in July).** The line: *"We're profitable; runway is infinite; we raise from strength."* ❌ Track actual cash-in-bank as a monthly line — it's the one number the sheets don't show.

### Contribution margin ⚠️
**Formula:** per-client monthly revenue − variable delivery cost for that client.
**Yours (Awish, July):** ₹60,000 − est. ₹25–30K allocated delivery ≈ **₹30–35K/month contribution**. ❌ Needs per-client cost allocation to be exact — add a "client" column to the expense sheet.

## 3. Growth & retention terms

### MoM growth ✅
**Formula:** (this month − last) ÷ last.
**Yours: May→Jun +94.9%, Jun→Jul +95.6%.** Two consecutive ~95% months. Always add the caveat "off a small base" — it buys credibility for the number.

### CAC ✅⚠️
**Formula:** sales + marketing spend ÷ new clients.
**Yours: ≈ ₹2,160** (₹6,479 total ads+sales spend ÷ 3 clients won). Reality: your clients came via referral/network, so blended CAC is near-zero. ⚠️ This won't hold at scale — track it monthly as paid channels grow.

### LTV ❌ (too early — use the formula, flag the data gap)
**Formula:** avg monthly contribution × avg client lifetime (months).
**Yours: insufficient history** (oldest client = 4 months, still active — a good sign, not yet a number). Awish LTV-to-date: ₹1,80,490 and counting. Quote LTV honestly as "TBD; oldest cohort still retained at month 4."

### LTV:CAC ⚠️
With CAC ≈ ₹2K and even one year of an average client (~₹25K+ contribution × 12 ≈ ₹3L), the ratio is **>100:1 on referral-sourced clients**. Quote the mechanism (referral flywheel) rather than the absurd ratio.

### CAC payback ✅
**Formula:** CAC ÷ monthly contribution.
**Yours: < 1 month.** Elite by any standard (SaaS norm 12–18 months).

### Churn ✅
**Formula:** clients (or revenue) lost ÷ starting base.
**Yours: 1 of 3 logos (Asset4u, refunded ₹6K = 2.5% of revenue).** Know the story: mismatched expectations, refunded fast, lesson = qualify harder. At 2 clients, churn of one = catastrophe — this is why concentration (below) is metric #1.

### GRR / NRR ✅ (your hidden weapon)
**Formula:** GRR = existing revenue retained (max 100%); NRR = retained + expansion.
**Yours: both active clients EXPANDED** — Awish ₹30,690 (May) → ₹60,000 (Jul); Savetime ₹19,000 → ₹38–40,000 within one month. Cohort-level **NRR is well above 150%** in this window. This is genuinely rare and is your best answer to "why will this scale?"

### Cohort analysis ✅ (trivial today, start the habit)
May cohort (Awish): month-1 ₹30.7K → month-3 ₹60K → retained. Jul cohort (Savetime): month-1 ₹57K → expanding. Keep this table monthly; at 20+ clients it becomes your Series-A slide.

### Concentration ✅ 🔴
**Yours: Awish = 76.0% of all revenue; top-2 = 100%.** The single riskiest number in the business and the first thing any investor sees. War-plan Q1's whole job: Awish under 40%.
