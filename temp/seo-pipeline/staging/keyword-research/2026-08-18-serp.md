## 3 content-gap opportunities

1. **No one covers UK AND Gulf in one piece.** Every result picks one corridor. A single "two-corridor" comparison (time zones, compliance, costs side-by-side) — today's article — has no direct competitor in these results.
2. **Nobody is salary-transparent.** All competitors quote bundled rates (£2,000/mo, AED 1,799/member) that hide what the worker earns. A "here's the expert's actual salary + our fee" breakdown page would be unique in this SERP and highly quotable by AI engines.
3. **Non-developer roles are ignored.** 90% of corridor content is developer-only. Dedicated marketers, video editors, SEO specialists for UK/Gulf SMEs (our actual case studies: Smile With Kris UK, Giant Migrations Gulf) is nearly uncontested — worth a role-by-corridor hub page.
