## 3 Content-Gap Opportunities

1. **"The third option" framing owns an empty position.** Every result is DIY-vs-agency or in-house-vs-agency. A comparison hub targeting "real estate marketing without an agency" that introduces the embedded-expert model as option 3 has no direct competitor in these SERPs. Today's article does this; a follow-up "real estate developer marketing team structure" piece would compound it.
2. **Cost-per-attended-site-visit calculator/content.** Pantheraa wins the India cost query by reframing the metric. AnantaSutra can one-up with "what ₹1L/month buys you: agency retainer vs dedicated marketer's salary" — using the Royal Properties +38% proof.
3. **India-specific in-house salary vs embedded cost data.** The in-house-vs-agency results are all US-centric ($55K–$85K). An India-salary-benchmarked comparison (clearly labeled as estimates) would be the only localized answer for this query cluster.
