## Content angles competitors use
- **Stats + 2026 recency** in titles ("Complete 2026 Guide") — nearly universal.
- **Concrete rate tables** — TechVinta wins the cost query by publishing actual numbers.
- **Definition-first** ("what a dedicated team is NOT") — Myra Technolabs.
- **Control/ownership framing** — The Scalers' "you own it, don't rent it."
- Almost all are **developer-only**. Nobody covers embedded *marketers/growth/design* offshore teams.

## 3 content-gap opportunities
1. **Non-developer embedded teams.** Every ranking page is dev-centric. "Offshore embedded marketing team India" / "dedicated growth expert India" is near-empty SERP territory — and it's AnantaSutra's proof-rich zone (Awish Rs25L→40L/60d, Zoom Wheels +46%).
2. **Salary-transparency pricing page.** Competitors publish blended hourly rates that hide margin. A page showing "actual salary + our flat fee = your cost" with role-by-role examples would be unique in this SERP and highly quotable by AI engines.
3. **SME-sized content.** Ranking guides assume 5–100 engineer ODCs. Nothing targets "1–3 embedded experts for a small business" — exactly the AnantaSutra ICP. A "1-person offshore team" angle (calculator + comparison vs freelancer vs agency) has no direct competition seen today.
