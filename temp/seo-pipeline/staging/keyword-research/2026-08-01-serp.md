## Query 3: "in-house marketing for medical practice vs agency"

Who appears: VMedX, Hooray Agency, Healthcare Success, Inbound Medic, Heno, Buzzbox Media, Whizzybly, Louisville Web Lab. All agencies writing self-serving comparisons. Notable data points seen in results (their claims, not verified): US in-house marketing manager $85K–140K/yr all-in; healthcare agency $48K–120K/yr; hybrid ~$200K/yr.

**Gap:** every comparison is binary (agency vs in-house employee). The embedded/dedicated-expert third option — full-time person, salary-only, vetting handled — is absent from all ranking content.
