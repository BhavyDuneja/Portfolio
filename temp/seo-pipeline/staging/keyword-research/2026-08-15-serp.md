## 3 Content-Gap Opportunities

1. **India-specific "true fully loaded cost" calculator/table content.** US math dominates query 1; only upGrowth/GUROB cover India, and neither includes the embedded-expert column. A 3-way India cost table (in-house vs agency vs embedded, in ₹) is an open gap — today's article starts this; a future interactive calculator page would compound it.
2. **Dedicated `/compare/` URL structure.** Cloud 9 Digital and EverestX rank with `/compare/agency-vs-in-house` style pages. AnantaSutra has no comparison-URL section. Opportunity: `/compare/in-house-vs-embedded`, `/compare/agency-vs-embedded`, `/compare/freelancer-vs-embedded`.
3. **"Salary-only" as a category term.** No one contests "pay just the salary" / "salary-only hiring model" in these SERPs. Consistent use of the phrase across articles + schema gives AnantaSutra a claimable category before staff-aug platforms notice it.
