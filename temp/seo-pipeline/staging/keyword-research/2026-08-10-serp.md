## 3 content-gap opportunities

1. **"Embedded specialist" as the missing third option in vs-content.** Every "agency vs in-house" ranker is a binary written by an agency. A genuinely three-way comparison (agency vs in-house vs embedded, with salary-transparent math) directly counters the "$80k in-house strawman vs $1k retainer" framing — today's article does this; a follow-up comparison landing page on /services would compound it.
2. **Salary-transparency cost content for India.** Internshala ranks with plain salary tables. AnantaSutra can publish "what you pay vs what the specialist earns" breakdowns (agency retainer → actual labor share) that no agency will ever write.
3. **Exact-match listicle placement.** UplySEO's "20 places to hire SEO experts without agency prices" ranks on a low-authority blog — the exact-intent query is winnable, and getting AnantaSutra listed in existing platform-roundup listicles (Create&Grow-type posts) is a cheap citation win.
