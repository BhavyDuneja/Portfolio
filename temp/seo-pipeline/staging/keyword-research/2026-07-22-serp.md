2. **Upwork** — freelance performance marketers marketplace — angle: volume marketplace, breadth
3. **Toptal** — "11 Best Freelance Performance Marketers" — angle: top-3% vetting, listicle format
4. **Uplers** — "Hire Performance Marketers ... Free Life-Time Replacement" — angle: India talent, replacement guarantee (direct competitor to our 48h-replacement claim)
5. **Somewhere.com** — "hire remote for 80% less than US salaries, 72 hours" — angle: cost-arbitrage + speed
6. Also present: PPCROY ("hire in 48 hours"), HireDigital

## Content angles in use

- Listicles ("11 best...") — Toptal
- Vs-pages (specialist vs agency) — EverestX, MarketerHire
- Pricing guides with concrete ranges — ClicksGeek
- Speed + guarantee claims (48–72h matching, lifetime replacement) — Uplers, PPCROY, Somewhere

## 3 Content-Gap Opportunities

1. **"Dedicated performance marketer vs agency retainer" true-cost calculator/breakdown** — everyone publishes agency pricing OR talent pricing; almost nobody puts salary+fee vs retainer side-by-side with a table and a worked example (we did this in today's article; a standalone /vs page would compound it).
2. **Vertical-specific dedicated-marketer pages** (clinics, real estate, dental) — SERP is generic; our proof (Awish, Royal Properties, Smile With Kris) lets us own "dedicated performance marketer for clinics" etc. with real numbers nobody else has.
3. **Replacement-guarantee comparison** — Uplers markets "life-time replacement," we offer 48h replacement. A transparent "what happens when your marketer isn't working out" page targets a real buyer anxiety no editorial content currently addresses head-on.
