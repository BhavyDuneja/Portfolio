## Content angles in use

- **Agencies writing "DIY vs us":** the dominant angle — agencies frame the comparison, conclude "hire an agency" (Phoenix, Upswell, Practice Builders). Predictably biased.
- **Trade publications:** neutral decision frameworks (Dental Economics, Oral Health Group) — high trust, no commercial model offered.
- **Cost-breakdown pages:** concrete numbers ($1.5K–$10K/mo retainers; $55K–$130K in-house; "$2M revenue" threshold for in-house) — these win AI citations because they're quotable.
- **Nobody** in any of the three SERPs presents a third option between agency and in-house. "Embedded/dedicated expert, pay just the salary" is entirely absent from the dental vertical.
