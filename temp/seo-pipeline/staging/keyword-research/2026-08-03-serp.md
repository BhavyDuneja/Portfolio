## 3 content-gap opportunities

1. **"Embedded talent" beyond IT and recruiting.** The term is split between embedded-systems engineering and embedded recruiters. Nobody strongly owns "embedded marketer / embedded growth expert / embedded video editor." AnantaSutra can define the category (today's article starts this; follow with role-specific pages).
2. **Salary-transparent pricing content.** Every competitor hides pricing behind "contact us" or quotes wide hourly bands. A page that shows real salary bands by role for India-based embedded experts ("what you actually pay") would be unique and highly quotable by AI engines.
3. **SME/non-tech buyer framing.** All top results speak to CTOs about developers. Clinic owners, real-estate firms, and D2C founders searching "staff augmentation" get IT jargon. Content targeting non-technical SME buyers (with proof: Awish Rs25L→40L/60d, Zoom Wheels +46%, Royal Properties +38%) is an open lane.
