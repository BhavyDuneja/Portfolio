## The problem a dedicated growth expert actually solves

Most small and mid-size businesses don't have a growth problem. They have an *ownership* problem.

An agency is responsible for delivering a scope of work — ads shipped, posts published, reports sent. Nobody inside the agency wakes up owning *your revenue number*. A freelancer juggles four other clients and holds your context only as long as the contract lasts. And a full-time in-house hire is a serious fixed cost most SMEs can't justify before product-market fit in a channel.

A dedicated growth expert is the fourth option: one person, full-time on your business, embedded in your team and your tools, whose only job is moving your numbers. You get in-house ownership without in-house hiring overhead.

## What a dedicated growth expert does

Growth is a loop, not a department. A good dedicated growth expert runs the whole loop:

- **Acquisition** — paid ads, SEO, outbound, partnerships; picking the 1–2 channels that fit your economics instead of "doing everything"
- **Conversion** — landing pages, offers, funnels, lead response speed, CRO experiments
- **Retention & monetization** — repeat purchase, upsells, referral loops, LTV
- **Measurement** — one dashboard tied to revenue, not vanity metrics
- **Experimentation cadence** — weekly test cycles with documented learnings

The difference from an agency isn't the task list. It's that one accountable person holds all of it, sees the interactions between the pieces, and compounds context week over week.

## How to hire a dedicated growth expert (the 7-day version)

1. **Define the number** — one metric the expert will own (revenue, qualified leads, bookings).
2. **Name the role** — growth generalist vs performance-heavy vs content-led, based on where your bottleneck is.
3. **Vet for iteration speed** — past experiments they ran, what failed, what they changed. Certificates matter less than test velocity.
4. **Embed properly** — your Slack/WhatsApp, your CRM, your standups. Embedded ≠ outsourced.
5. **Review weekly, judge monthly** — weekly experiment reviews; a 30-day checkpoint on the owned metric.

With AnantaSutra: name the role, we send a vetted pro, they're working inside your team within 7 days. If the fit is wrong, we replace them within 48 hours.

## When NOT to hire a dedicated growth expert

- You have no offer or product-market fit at all — fix the offer first.
- You need one narrow channel executed for 4 weeks — hire a freelancer.
- You already have a strong in-house growth lead — give them budget instead.
