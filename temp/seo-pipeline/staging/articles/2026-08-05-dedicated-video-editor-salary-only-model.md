## What "dedicated video editor" actually means

Three words, three commitments:

- **Dedicated** — the editor works only for you. Not a pool, not a queue. Your projects are their entire job.
- **Embedded** — they sit inside your workflow: your Slack/WhatsApp, your Google Drive or Frame.io, your daily standup if you want. You direct the work.
- **Full-time** — 160+ hours a month. Volume stops being a pricing question; it becomes a scheduling question.

This is the same "embedded talent" structure used in [staff augmentation](https://kitrum.com/blog/embedded-staff-augmentation/) for engineers — you keep management control, the partner handles sourcing, vetting, and payroll. AnantaSutra applies it to creative and marketing roles: you tell us the skillset (short-form reels, YouTube long-form, motion graphics, ad creatives), we place a vetted editor inside your team in about 7 days.

That transparency is the whole point.

## Freelancer vs. subscription vs. dedicated: quick answer

- **Choose a freelancer** if you produce under ~4 videos a month or are still experimenting with formats.
- **Choose a subscription/unlimited service** if you have bursty, unpredictable volume and don't care who edits.
- **Choose a dedicated editor** if you publish consistently (8+ videos/month), care about a recognizable style, and are tired of re-briefing. As [GrowthAssistant's comparison](https://growthassistant.com/article/video-editor-vs-freelancer-guide) notes, without months of repeated work a freelancer never learns your pacing, revision patterns, or creative voice. A dedicated editor learns them once and compounds.

The hidden cost people miss: sourcing, briefing, and QA-ing a new editor for every project is *your* time. At founder hourly value, that overhead often exceeds the editing bill.

## What to look for when you hire

- **Show, don't tell:** a test edit on *your* raw footage beats any showreel.
- **Stack fit:** Premiere Pro dominates; After Effects for motion graphics; DaVinci for color-heavy work.
- **Turnaround SLAs in writing:** e.g., 48h first cut on short-form, 2 revision rounds included.
- **Async discipline:** if the editor is in India and you're in the UK/Gulf, overlap hours and a Frame.io/Notion review loop matter more than time zone.
- **Replacement terms:** if the provider won't commit to a fast swap, you're carrying all the risk.
