## The problem: you never know what the person actually costs

Ask any agency or staff-aug vendor a simple question: "How much of what I pay actually reaches the person doing my work?"

You won't get a straight answer. Here's why.

Industry data on staff augmentation pricing shows vendors typically add **30-50% markup on top of developer salary** for IT and software roles — and when you count every layer (statutory costs, vendor overhead, account management, profit margin), total markup on the worker's pay runs **25-75%** ([Full Scale](https://fullscale.io/blog/staff-augmentation-pricing-model/), [Nearshore Business Solutions](https://nearshorebusinesssolutions.com/news/staff-augmentation-costs/)). Traditional staffing agencies mark up wages anywhere from **25% to 100%** ([altLINE](https://altline.sobanco.com/how-staffing-agencies-make-money/)).

Recruitment agencies in India charge 8.33% of annual salary just for a one-time placement — rising to 15-25% for specialized roles ([Wisemonk](https://www.wisemonk.io/blogs/cost-of-hiring-in-india)). And marketplace platforms bill "blended figures you cannot see inside" — the client rate and the talent's actual pay are deliberately kept apart ([Teilur Talent comparison](https://www.teilurtalent.com/insights/toptal-vs-turing-vs-teilur-talent-pricing-true-costs-compared)).

So when you pay an agency ₹1,50,000/month for a "dedicated marketer," the person doing your work might be seeing half of it. The other half funds the vendor's sales team, office, and margin. That's not evil — it's just their business model. But it's *your* money.

## What "pay just the salary" actually means

The model has three rules:

1. **The salary is visible.** You know what the expert earns. There's no blended rate.
2. **The fee is flat and small.** Whatever the provider charges sits openly on top — not buried inside an hourly rate.
3. **The expert works for you.** Full-time, in your Slack, your standups, your tools. Not a shared resource juggling six accounts.

This is different from three things people confuse it with:

| Model | What you pay | What you get | Hidden markup? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Traditional agency | Retainer (₹50K-3L+/mo) | Shared team, 4-6 hrs/week of real attention | Yes — you fund their whole office |
| Freelance marketplace | Hourly blended rate | Fractional attention, context lost between gigs | Yes — platform takes a cut you don't see |
| Staff-aug vendor | Salary + 30-50% markup | Dedicated person, opaque pricing | Usually — markup rarely disclosed |
| **Pay-just-the-salary** | **Salary + small flat fee** | **Dedicated, embedded, full-time expert** | **No — everything is on the table** |

A few global platforms have moved this direction — Braintrust lets talent keep 100% of their rate with a flat ~10-15% client fee on top ([Useme](https://useme.com/en/blog/toptal-alternatives/)), and transparency is now a real differentiator against blended-rate networks like Toptal ([Teilur Talent](https://www.teilurtalent.com/insights/toptal-vs-turing-vs-teilur-talent-pricing-true-costs-compared)). AnantaSutra applies the same principle to embedded experts for Indian and global SMEs — across marketing, engineering, design, and operations, not just developers.

## Why this model wins on math, not marketing

Run the numbers on a mid-level performance marketer in India:

- **Agency route:** ₹60K-1.5L/month retainer. Your account gets a few hours a week from a shared team. Estimate — agencies don't publish attention-per-client.
- **Pay-just-the-salary route:** the same calibre marketer's salary (visible to you) plus a small flat fee. The person works only on your business, 40+ hours a week.

Per hour of actual expert attention on *your* business, the embedded model is usually several times cheaper — because you're not buying the vendor's overhead, you're buying the person.

The continuity argument matters just as much as cost. Freelancers juggle multiple clients, so context is lost between sessions and every engagement forces them to relearn your product ([Foundey](https://foundey.com/blog/embedded-design-team-vs-hiring)). An embedded expert compounds: month 3 output beats month 1 because they know your customers, your numbers, your history.

## FAQ

**Q: What is the pay just the salary talent model?**
A: A hiring model where you get a full-time dedicated expert embedded in your team and pay their actual salary plus a small flat fee — instead of an agency retainer or a blended rate hiding a 30-50% markup.

**Q: How is it different from staff augmentation?**
A: Structurally similar — the difference is pricing transparency. Staff-aug vendors typically add an undisclosed 30-50% markup on salary; in the pay-just-the-salary model the salary and fee are shown separately.

**Q: Is it cheaper than a marketing agency?**
A: Per hour of real attention on your business, usually yes — an agency retainer buys you a slice of a shared team, while here the full salary buys 100% of one expert's time. Exact savings depend on role and seniority.

**Q: What if the expert doesn't work out?**
A: AnantaSutra replaces the expert within 48 hours. You don't restart a hiring cycle.

**Q: Do I manage the expert or does AnantaSutra?**
A: You do — that's the point. The expert joins your standups and reports to you, like an employee, without your payroll/compliance burden.

**Q: How fast can I get someone?**
A: Typically live in 7 days from role definition.
