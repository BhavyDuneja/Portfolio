## When the agency is actually right

Agencies earn their keep when the work is genuinely campaign-shaped: a product launch, a rebrand, a one-time website build. You rent an assembled team for a defined sprint and walk away. The model breaks when owners use agencies for *continuous* growth work — that's when the retainer meter runs forever, your account becomes one of twenty, and the monthly report gets thinner while the invoice doesn't. If you've ever paid ₹1L/month and struggled to name what changed in your revenue, you've felt this.

## When in-house is right

Hire in-house when the function is proven and permanent: you know exactly what a marketer or developer produces for you monthly, and you need more than one of them. In-house wins on culture, context and compounding knowledge. Its cost is the path to get there — recruiter fees (often 8–16% of CTC), 3–6 months including notice periods, and the full misfire risk: a wrong senior hire in India typically costs a quarter's salary plus another quarter of lost time.

## When embedded wins

The embedded model exists for the middle — which is where most owner-led businesses actually live:

- You need **full-time dedication** (an agency can't give it) but can't spend **6 months hiring** (in-house can't move that fast).
- You want the money to reach **the person doing the work**: you pay the expert's salary, see the coordination fee as a line item, and skip the 30–50% markup entirely.

## FAQ

**Q: Is an embedded expert the same as staff augmentation?**
A: Structurally similar, philosophically different. Staff-aug firms typically add a 40%+ margin inside an opaque bill rate and focus on IT bodies. The embedded model shows you the salary and the fee separately, covers 12+ domains, and includes accountability mechanisms like weekly reviews and a 48-hour replacement guarantee.

**Q: What does an embedded expert cost compared to an agency retainer?**
A: A mid-level dedicated marketer in India earns roughly ₹50K–₹1L/month (est.). A multi-channel agency retainer commonly runs ₹1–4.5L/month with shared attention. For the same or less money, embedded gives you 160+ dedicated hours instead of retainer slices.

**Q: Can I convert an embedded expert to an in-house employee later?**
A: Often, yes — a good partner treats that as a graduation, not a betrayal. Terms (notice and transition) should be agreed upfront.

**Q: Is in-house always better long-term?**
A: Once you need 3+ permanent people in a function, usually yes. Until then, in-house means you carry recruiting cost, ramp time, and misfire risk for a function you're still validating.

**Q: What if the embedded expert doesn't perform?**
A: That's the model's core test. At AnantaSutra the answer is contractual: replacement within 48 hours, no lock-in, and a 90-day scoped target so "performing" is a number, not a feeling.
