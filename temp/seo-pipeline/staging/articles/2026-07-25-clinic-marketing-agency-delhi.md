## What you get — and don't get — from a clinic marketing agency

**What you get:** a process that's been run on many clinics before, faster start than hiring, and packaged reporting.

**What you don't get:**

- **Full-time attention.** Your account manager runs 8–15 clinics. Yours gets a few hours a week.
- **Someone inside your clinic.** Nobody from the agency hears your front desk lose a lead, sees your OPD flow, or knows which doctor converts consults best.
- **The margin back.** Of every rupee you pay, a large slice funds the agency's office, sales team and profit — not work on your clinic.
- **Continuity.** Agency-side churn means re-explaining your clinic to a new executive every few months.
