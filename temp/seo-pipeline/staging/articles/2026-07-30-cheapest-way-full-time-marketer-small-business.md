## "Cheapest" is a per-dedicated-hour question

Owners compare monthly invoices. That's the wrong denominator. The right one is **cost per dedicated hour of skilled work on your business**. A ₹40K freelancer who gives you 30 hours a month is ₹1,333/hour. A ₹1.2L agency retainer that nets ~25 hours of actual specialist time across a juggled team is ₹4,800/hour. A ₹70K embedded marketer working only for you (~170 hours) is ₹412/hour. Same "marketing budget" conversation, 10x difference in what you actually receive.

## Why the "cheap" options cost small businesses the most

**The ₹15K intern-plus-Canva route.** The most common small-business choice and the most expensive per result: you now personally manage someone who is learning on your revenue. Six months later the Instagram has posts and the clinic has no new patients.

**The cousin/part-timer route.** Free until you calculate the opportunity cost of a year of flat sales. Awish Clinic ran on exactly this before switching — with an embedded web-CRM-ads pod, monthly revenue went from Rs 25L to Rs 40L in 60 days. The "free" years were the expensive ones.

**The cheapest-agency route.** A ₹25K retainer buys you a template calendar and a monthly PDF. Vanity metrics aren't cheap at any price.

## How to actually do it (5 steps)

1. **Write the outcome line:** "₹X in leads/revenue from channel Y in 90 days." If you can't write it, you're not ready to spend on any model.
2. **Pick the specialist that matches your #1 channel** — performance ads, SEO/content, or funnel/CRM. One specialist beats one generalist at small-business scale (Zoom Wheels: +46% revenue with one dedicated ads expert; Royal Properties: +38% traffic with funnel specialists).
3. **Choose your risk-holder.** If you have a quarter to spare and hiring muscle, go in-house. If you want the vetting, speed and replacement guarantee handled, go embedded — you pay just the salary either way.
4. **Interview 2–3 vetted profiles** (at AnantaSutra this shortlist is standard). Interview for ownership: good candidates ask about your margins before your ad budget.
5. **Onboard like an employee:** company email, standups, WhatsApp, a 30-day scorecard with 2–3 numbers.

## FAQ

**Q: What is the absolute minimum budget for a real full-time marketer in India?**
A: Realistically ~₹40–50K/month at junior-mid level (est.). Below that you're buying part-time attention or trainee output, whatever the title says.

**Q: Isn't a freelancer cheaper than an embedded expert?**
A: Sometimes on invoice, rarely on dedication. Freelancers split attention across clients and carry no replacement guarantee. Embedded costs salary + a small fee and gives you 100% of the person's working month.

**Q: Why don't agencies just charge salary-level prices?**
A: Their model can't — office, account managers, sales and margin sit inside the retainer. That overhead is the 30–50% markup; removing the middle layer is exactly why the embedded model is cheaper per hour.

**Q: Can a small business start with less than one full-time person?**
A: Yes — but decide deliberately: a fractional senior (strategy-heavy, hands-light) or a full-time mid-level embedded marketer (hands-heavy). Most sub-₹10Cr businesses need hands first.

**Q: What proof exists that the embedded model works for small businesses?**
A: Documented client outcomes: Awish Clinic Rs 25L→Rs 40L monthly revenue in 60 days; Zoom Wheels +46% revenue; Royal Properties +38% traffic — each with one dedicated embedded specialist or a small pod, paid at salary.
