## Why real estate marketing agencies underdeliver

Not because agencies are evil. Because of how the retainer model is structured:

- **Your account is one of many.** A typical account executive handles 6–12 clients. Your Gurgaon project gets a few hours a week of actual attention.
- **The fee structure rewards spend, not bookings.** Many real estate agencies charge a percentage of ad spend or a flat retainer. Industry pricing guides put Indian retainers at roughly ₹1.5L–₹6L/month for SMBs, with performance-marketing programs starting around ₹98K/month — before a rupee goes to Meta or Google ([Pantheraa](https://pantheraa.com/blog/real-estate-marketing-cost-india.html), [OneDizitalz](https://onedizitalz.in/digital-marketing-agency-cost-india/)).
- **Leads ≠ site visits.** The metric that matters in real estate is cost per attended site visit, not cost per form fill. Agencies report the number that looks good; the number that closes flats is different.
- **Templates over context.** The gap between a ₹60K and ₹2L retainer is usually who touches your account — a junior following a template, or a senior strategist. You rarely know which one you're getting.

We saw this firsthand: **Royal Properties**, a real estate client, moved to a dedicated embedded marketer under AnantaSutra's model and grew qualified inquiries by **38%** — not because of a secret tactic, but because one accountable person worked their funnel daily instead of weekly.

## Option 1: Full DIY

Doing it yourself is genuinely viable at the individual-agent level. Low-budget playbooks focus on geographic farming (consistent presence in a 500–1,000 home area), smartphone property-tour videos, and a simple IDX website ([Blastrow](https://www.blastrow.com/blog/low-budget-real-estate-marketing-strategies-for-agents-in-2026), [Luxury Presence](https://www.luxurypresence.com/blogs/real-estate-marketing-ideas/)).

The catch: running social, email, SEO, and paid ads manually takes **15–20 hours per week minimum** ([AI HomeDesign](https://aihomedesign.com/blog/real-estate-marketing/real-estate-marketing-agency/)). If your time is better spent closing bookings and negotiating with landowners, DIY is the most expensive "free" option there is.

**DIY fits:** solo agents, sub-₹50L ad budgets of zero, early brand-building.
**DIY breaks:** developers with active project launches, anyone running paid campaigns.

## Option 2: In-house marketing hire

Building in-house gives you full control and brand depth. It also costs more than most owners expect: a competent marketing manager runs $55,000–$85,000/year in Western markets plus 20–30% for benefits and $5K–$15K in tools ([Laire Digital](https://www.lairedigital.com/blog/hire-marketing-employee-vs-marketing-agency)). In India, a good real estate performance marketer with 4+ years of experience commands ₹8L–₹15L+ annually (estimate — verify against current salary data), plus recruitment time of 60–90 days, plus the risk that one bad hire sets your launch back a quarter.

And one person rarely covers the full stack: performance ads, creative, landing pages, CRM follow-up, and portal listings are four different skill sets.

**In-house fits:** developers with multiple simultaneous projects and ₹5Cr+ marketing budgets.
**In-house breaks:** anyone who can't afford a 90-day hiring cycle and a wrong-hire write-off.
