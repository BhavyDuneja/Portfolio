## H1: In-House Marketer vs Embedded Expert — What Each One Actually Costs

Most founders compare the wrong numbers. They look at an in-house marketer's salary, compare it to an agency retainer, and stop there. The real comparison has four layers: acquisition cost, carrying cost, risk cost, and switching cost. Let's run all four honestly.

## What an in-house marketer really costs in India (2026)

Published salary bands for Indian marketing roles (third-party figures from [upGrowth's 2026 cost guide](https://upgrowth.in/digital-marketer-cost-india/) — estimates, not verified by us):

| Role | Annual salary band (India) |
|---|---|
| SEO specialist | ₹3,00,000 – ₹8,00,000 |
| Performance marketer | ₹6,00,000 – ₹18,00,000 |
| Content strategist | ₹5,00,000 – ₹14,00,000 |
| Graphic designer | ₹4,00,000 – ₹8,00,000 |

So a bare-bones four-person team is roughly ₹18L–₹48L/year in base salary alone. But salary is only the visible layer. Industry analyses consistently put the fully loaded cost of a full-time employee at **30–50% above salary** once you add:

- **Recruitment:** job portal fees, recruiter commissions (typically 8.33% of CTC via agencies), and 45–90 days of hiring time — during which nothing gets marketed.
- **Benefits and statutory costs:** PF, gratuity, insurance, paid leave.
- **Tools:** a working marketing stack (CRM, SEO platform, email automation, design software, analytics) runs $15,000–$40,000/year for a small team per US-market estimates ([Digital Osmos SMB cost breakdown](https://digitalosmos.com/blog-in-house-marketing-vs-outsourcing-cost-breakdown-smbs/) — estimate).
- **Management:** someone senior has to review the work. If that's you, it's founder hours — your most expensive resource.
- **Attrition risk:** if your marketer quits at month 8, you restart the entire cycle. Hiring cost, training cost, and 2–3 months of lost momentum are all repeated.

**Realistic fully loaded cost of one mid-level in-house performance marketer in India: ₹10L–₹24L/year**, not the ₹6L–₹18L on the offer letter.

## The mistake that costs the most

The most expensive option isn't the in-house hire or the agency — it's **churning between models**. Fire an agency, spend 3 months hiring in-house, watch the hire quit, sign another agency. Every switch costs 60–90 days of momentum. That's why we built the 48-hour replacement guarantee: the model should absorb people-risk, not transfer it to you.

## How to decide in 10 minutes

1. **Count the real monthly workload.** Under ~20 hrs/month → freelancer. 40+ hrs of one skill → full-time attention is justified.
2. **Compute the fully loaded in-house number.** Salary × 1.3–1.5, plus tools, plus your management hours.
3. **Ask who carries the attrition risk.** In-house: you. Embedded: the partner.
4. **Check for lock-ins.** Any model you can't exit in 30 days should be priced as a risk.

If full-time attention is justified but the fully loaded in-house number makes you wince, the embedded model exists precisely for that gap.
