## When You Actually Need a Dedicated Manager (Not a Freelancer)

A useful threshold cited in hiring guides: once you're handling roughly 500+ monthly social interactions (comments, DMs, mentions), manual management becomes a full-time job (per [Wishup's hiring guide](https://www.wishup.co/blog/hire-social-media-manager/) — their benchmark, not ours). Other signals:

- Posting has become inconsistent because "whoever has time" does it
- You're on 3+ platforms and each needs native formats (Reels, LinkedIn docs, Shorts)
- Leads come in via DMs and nobody replies within hours
- Your agency sends a monthly report you don't read, for work you can't see

If only one of these is true, a freelancer might do. If three are true, you need someone who wakes up thinking about your brand.
