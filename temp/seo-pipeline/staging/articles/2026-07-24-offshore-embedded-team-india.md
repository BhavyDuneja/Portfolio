## What "offshore embedded team" actually means

Three words, three claims:

- **Offshore** — the talent sits in India. That's where the cost advantage comes from: industry guides put Indian rates at roughly 60–70% below equivalent US hires.
- **Embedded** — they work *inside* your company. Your Slack, your standups, your sprint board, your priorities. Industry definitions are consistent on this: augmented staff "operate under the client company's management and direction, integrate into existing workflows and tools" ([Cleveroad](https://www.cleveroad.com/blog/staff-augmentation/), [Oyster](https://www.oysterhr.com/glossary/staff-augmentation)).
- **Team (or expert)** — full-time and dedicated. Not a freelancer juggling five clients. Not an agency pod shared across accounts. As one 2026 guide puts it: a dedicated team "works exclusively on your product, under your direction, using your processes" ([Myra Technolabs](https://www.myratechnolabs.com/blog/hire-dedicated-development-team-india/)).

The single biggest difference vs. an outsourcing agency is **control**. With outsourcing, the vendor manages delivery and you get low control. With embedded talent, you retain high control and manage the person directly ([BairesDev](https://www.bairesdev.com/blog/staff-augmentation/)).

## The three ways to build an offshore team in India

**1. Outsourcing agency.** Fastest to start, least control. You brief; they deliver; resources are shared across clients. Works for one-off projects. Breaks down when you need ongoing ownership — the vendor's culture gets imposed on the team, not yours ([The Scalers](https://thescalers.com/how-to-build-an-odc-team-in-india/)).

**2. Set up your own entity / ODC.** Maximum control, maximum overhead. You handle Indian company registration, compliance, payroll, office, HR. Sensible at 20+ heads. Overkill for an SME that needs 1–5 experts.

**3. Embedded talent partner (the middle path).** A partner recruits, vets, and payrolls; the expert works exclusively for you, under your direction. You get ODC-level control without ODC-level admin. This is AnantaSutra's model.

## How to run an embedded offshore team well

1. **Hire for the role, not the résumé keyword.** Define the outcome (e.g., "own paid acquisition, target CPL under ₹X") before screening.
2. **Insist on time-zone overlap.** 3–4 hours of shared working time is the minimum for real embedding.
3. **Onboard like an employee.** Day-one access to tools, a named internal buddy, week-one goals. The model fails when companies treat embedded talent like vendors.
4. **Weekly metrics review, not monthly reports.** Embedded experts should sit in the same dashboards you do.
5. **Demand a replacement guarantee.** People-risk is real. AnantaSutra's standard is a 48-hour replacement; anything slower than a week should be a red flag.
