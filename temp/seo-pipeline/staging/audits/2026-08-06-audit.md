- **Meta description:** Present, strong — mentions salary-only pricing, no 30-50% markup, 7-day onboarding, 48h replacement. Slightly over 155 chars; Google may truncate tail.
- **H1:** "Not a project. A dedicated expert." + "InfiniteWisdomApplied" — two H1-level texts detected; ideally one H1 per page.
- **Heading structure:** H2s — "The Endless Thread of Knowledge," "Any expert you need, working for you," "Numbers That Speak," "Let's Weave Your Thread." H3s cover process steps and value props. Logical hierarchy overall.
- **Word count:** ~1,000–1,200 words. Adequate for a homepage.
- **Alt text:** Mandala/geometry background image lacks descriptive alt text; logos are labeled.
- **Schema:** Organization, WebSite, and founder (Bhavya Duneja) structured data present. Good entity foundation.
- **Viewport meta:** Present (`width=device-width, initial-scale=1`). Mobile-ready.
- **Navigation:** Home, Services, Portfolio, Apps, About, Blog, Contact, Get Started.
