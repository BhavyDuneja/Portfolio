## Top 3 prioritized fixes

1. **Rewrite /services title tag + meta description to the dedicated-experts positioning** (e.g., "Dedicated Experts in Marketing, Dev & Design | AnantaSutra" ≤60 chars). Right now the SERP snippet sells a business model the site no longer leads with — this misaligns every click AND every AI-engine citation.
2. **Update the Service JSON-LD on /services** — remove/replace the ₹6/min voice-agent and ₹2/lead recruiter-AI offer items with role-based service entries (dedicated marketer, dedicated developer, etc.). AI engines read schema literally; current schema tells them AnantaSutra is an AI-tools vendor.
3. **Add FAQPage schema to the homepage** (or a linked FAQ section) covering "what is an embedded expert", "how does pay-just-the-salary work", "how fast can we start" — these map directly to the geoQuestions the content pipeline targets and increase answer-engine quotability.
