## Notes

- **Body vs metadata mismatch on /services:** page body now reflects the embedded-experts model, but title tag, meta description, and Service schema still sell the old AI/SaaS offering. This confuses both Google snippets and AI answer engines about what AnantaSutra is.
- Sitemap/robots: not fetched today (previous audits confirmed presence via `app/sitemap.ts` in repo); not re-verified — carry-over item.
- Alt text completeness: not verifiable from rendered fetch — needs code-level check of image components.

## 3 Prioritized Fixes

1. **P1 — Rewrite /services title tag + meta description + Service JSON-LD to the dedicated-experts positioning.** Current metadata actively contradicts the homepage and the brand's entity definition. Suggested title (≤60 chars): "Hire Dedicated Experts — Pay Just the Salary | AnantaSutra".
2. **P2 — Trim homepage title to ≤60 chars and meta description to ≤155.** E.g., "Dedicated Experts, Embedded in Your Team | AnantaSutra" (54 chars).
3. **P3 — Add a keyworded H2 near the top of the homepage** (e.g., "Hire a dedicated marketer, developer or designer — pay just the salary") so at least one crawlable heading carries commercial search terms; the current H1/H2 set is brand-voice only.
