## Top 3 prioritized fixes

1. **Rewrite `/services` title tag + meta description to the dedicated-experts model.** The metadata still sells AI automation while the H1 sells embedded experts — this confuses both Google and AI answer engines about what the entity is. E.g. title: "Hire Dedicated Experts — Pay Just the Salary | AnantaSutra" (58 chars).
2. **Shorten homepage title tag to ≤60 chars** and add one keyword-bearing H2 (e.g., "Hire a dedicated expert — pay just the salary") so the homepage can rank for commercial queries, not just brand.
3. **Add descriptive alt text sitewide** (services page images have none; homepage decorative images missing alt). Quick accessibility + image-SEO win.
