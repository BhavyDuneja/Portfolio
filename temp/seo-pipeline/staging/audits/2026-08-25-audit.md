3. **Add one keyword-bearing H2 on the homepage** (e.g. "Hire a dedicated marketer, developer, or designer — pay just the salary") and descriptive alt text on the mandala background image. Also: create a landing section/page for paid-media ("Dedicated Google Ads / Performance Marketing Specialist") — the role is listed but has zero dedicated content to rank or be cited.
