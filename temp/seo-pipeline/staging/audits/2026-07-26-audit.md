## Top 3 prioritized fixes

1. **Rewrite `/services` title tag + meta description** to the dedicated-experts positioning (e.g., "Dedicated Experts in Any Domain — Pay Just the Salary | AnantaSutra"). Highest impact: this is the page most likely to be cited for service-intent queries, and it currently contradicts the brand.
