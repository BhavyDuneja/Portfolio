## Top 3 prioritized fixes

1. **`/services` metadata + Service schema still sell the old AI products.** Title, meta description, and JSON-LD advertise "AI voice agents ₹6/min, recruiter AI ₹2/lead" while the visible page says "Experts On Demand." This is a positioning conflict Google and AI engines will index verbatim — AI Overviews may describe AnantaSutra as an AI-automation vendor. Rewrite title/meta/Service schema around embedded experts / pay-just-the-salary. (Highest impact, small effort.)
2. **Trim homepage title (~73→≤60 chars) and meta description (~230→≤155 chars)** so the markup-free value prop ("Pay Just the Salary") survives truncation. E.g., title: "Dedicated Embedded Experts — Pay Just the Salary | AnantaSutra".
3. **Add alt text to non-decorative images and add keyword-bearing H2s.** Homepage H2s ("The Endless Thread of Knowledge") carry zero search intent. Keep the voice, but add one section H2 matching a query pattern (e.g., "Hire a dedicated expert without agency markup"). Also add FAQPage schema — the site has none, and it's the cheapest GEO win available.
