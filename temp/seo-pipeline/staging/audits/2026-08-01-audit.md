## Top 3 prioritized fixes

1. **Rewrite /services title tag + meta description to the dedicated-experts positioning** (e.g., "Dedicated Experts in Any Domain — Pay Just the Salary | AnantaSutra"). This is the highest-traffic mismatch: SERP snippet currently sells the abandoned AI/SaaS model. Also review the Offer schema (₹6/min voice agents) — it feeds AI engines the old model.
2. **Fix /blog listing page rendering:** ensure post titles/excerpts are server-rendered (SSR/SSG) with an H1 and `Blog` + `ItemList` schema. Right now the listing gives crawlers nothing to index, which throttles discovery of the 17 articles produced by this pipeline.
3. **Add one keyword-bearing H2 on the homepage** (e.g., "The agency alternative: dedicated embedded experts") and trim the title tag to ≤60 chars. Brand-poetry H2s are fine, but at least one heading should match what buyers search.
