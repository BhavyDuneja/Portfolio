# SERP & Competitor Watch — 2026-07-15

**Target:** "hire dedicated marketer" + related: "hire dedicated marketing expert for small business cost", "dedicated marketer vs marketing agency which is better"

*Source: live web search results observed today. No paid rank-tracking tools used — positions are as returned by search, volumes not available (not verified).*

## Who ranks — "hire dedicated marketer"
1. **MarketerHire** (marketerhire.com) — talent-marketplace homepage. Angle: speed ("hire in <1 week") + vetting + 2-week trial.
2. **Arc.dev** (/hire-marketers) — remote freelance marketplace. Angle: "hire in 72 hours", direct connection to vetted marketers.
3. **letsremotify** — step-by-step hiring guide (blog). Angle: educational how-to.
4. **Alltalentz / Kbizsoft / HireADev** — offshore "hire dedicated digital marketing expert" service pages. Angle: dedicated-staff pricing, India-based delivery.
5. **Toptal** (/marketing/digital) — premium freelance network listicle.

## Who ranks — cost query
- LyfeMarketing (agency cost guide), business.com, 7 Mile Media (cost/models/ROI explainer), Wayfind Marketing ("true cost of an agency"), Founders Network (startup hiring guide). Angle: cost-breakdown editorial content with ranges ($1.5K–$5K/mo agency; ~$85K/yr in-house; $25–150/hr freelance).

## Who ranks — comparison query
- Forbes Agency Council, Laire Digital, Intergrowth (cost breakdown), Black Propeller, Breef, WebTek. Angle: balanced "agency vs in-house" pros/cons; almost all conclude "hybrid" — and notably, **HireADev already ranks with "dedicated marketer vs consultative agency"**, the closest competitor framing to AnantaSutra's.

## Content angles in use
- Marketplaces: speed + vetting + trial guarantees (commercial pages).
- Agencies/blogs: cost-transparency editorial ("what you'll really pay").
- Comparison posts: agency vs in-house binary; embedded/dedicated as a third option is barely represented outside recruitment-industry content.

## 3 Content-Gap Opportunities
1. **"Third option" comparison content.** Nearly all ranking comparisons are binary (agency vs in-house). A definitive "Agency vs In-house vs Embedded Dedicated Expert" page with a cost table and decision matrix fills a visible gap (also matches a queued geoQuestion).
2. **Transparent total-cost calculator/breakdown for India-based dedicated marketers.** Cost pages ranking today are US-centric ($85K/yr framing). An honest INR/USD "what a dedicated marketer really costs via embedded model vs agency retainer" piece has no strong incumbent in the results observed.
3. **Proof-led vertical pages.** Ranking players show generic vetting claims, not documented client numbers. Pages like "dedicated marketer for clinics" leveraging Awish (Rs 25L→40L/60d) target long-tail commercial queries the marketplaces don't localize.
