## 1. Core Editing Craft 🔴 MUST

The foundation everything else sits on.

- **Cutting & pacing** — cuts on action and intent; keeps a 30-second reel moving with a visual change every 2–3 seconds; kills dead air instinctively without making the edit feel rushed.
- **Story structure** — internalizes the **hook → beats → CTA** structure (the exact format of AnantaSutra reel scripts). Can read a script once and *see* the edit — which line lands on which shot, where the text pops, where the cut breathes.
- **J-cuts & L-cuts** — audio leads or trails the video for natural conversational flow in talking-head content. If they don't know what these are by name, they must demonstrate them by instinct.
- **Retention editing** — pattern interrupts (zoom punch-ins, angle switches, b-roll inserts, speed ramps) timed to hold attention past the 3-second mark and through the mid-point drop-off.
- **Continuity & rhythm** — matching action across cuts, consistent eyeline, no jump-cut jarring unless intentional and stylistic.

**Red flag:** an editor who opens the timeline and starts cutting before reading the script.

---

## 2. Short-Form / Reels Mastery 🔴 MUST — *this is 80% of the job*

- **9:16 native thinking** — frames, composes and places text for vertical first; never letterboxes a horizontal edit and calls it a reel.
- **Safe zones** — text and key visuals never sit under the caption bar, username overlay, or right-side action buttons. Knows the current IG/LinkedIn UI margins by heart.
- **Captions (critical):**
  - Karaoke-style word-by-word or phrase-by-phrase subtitles, timed tightly to speech.
  - **Hinglish accuracy** — auto-captioning tools reliably butcher Hindi-English mixed speech ("bilkul", "lakhs", "chai pe"). Every line must be manually proofed. A single wrong caption on a founder video damages credibility.
  - Consistent caption style across all videos: same font, size, stroke/shadow, highlight color (saffron), position.
- **On-screen text beats** — our scripts specify them explicitly (e.g. `On-screen text: DAY 63. STILL OPEN.`). The editor places, times and styles these to brand, treating them as first-class content, not decoration.
- **Trend literacy with judgment** — follows current reel formats, transitions and audio trends, **and** has the taste to know which fit a premium B2B founder brand and which would look cheap. We are "calm, sharp, numbers-driven" — never scream-edits, never 15 effects in 20 seconds.
- **Hook obsession** — treats the first 3 seconds as the entire job. Tests alternative hook cuts when the recorded take is weak.

---

## 3. Audio 🔴 MUST

Audiences forgive average video; they never forgive bad audio.

- **Voice cleanup** — noise reduction, de-reverb, EQ, compression and levelling so phone-recorded founder audio sounds near-studio. Target: consistent loudness (~-14 LUFS for social).
- **Music selection** — background tracks that match the mood of the script (confident, warm, modern — not corporate elevator, not hype-trap unless the format demands it). Uses platform-safe / licensed libraries only.
- **Ducking** — music auto-ducks under speech and swells in gaps; never fights the voice.
- **Beat-syncing** — cuts land on beats where the format calls for it (montages, b-roll runs).
- **Silence & filler removal** — tightens "um", "uh", long pauses — by hand or with AI tools (Descript-style) — without chopping the natural rhythm of speech.

---

## 4. Brand System Discipline 🔴 MUST — *non-negotiable at AnantaSutra*

The feed must look like **one brand**, not a collection of one-off edits.

- **Brand palette (memorize):**
  | Use | Color | Hex |
  |---|---|---|
  | Primary accent / highlights / CTA | Saffron | `#E8A317` |
  | Secondary accent | Violet | `#6A3DE8` |
  | Text on light | Warm ink | `#1F1812` |
  | Light background | Off-white | `#FBF5E5` |
  | Dark background | Near-black | `#0A0A0F` |
- **Typography** — modern geometric sans (Segoe UI / Inter family look). No script fonts, no Comic-style, no default CapCut yellow.
- **Logo** — AnantaSutra wordmark bottom-right, subtle, consistent size and margin. Never animated obnoxiously.
- **Voice & tone** — knows the tagline *"Not a project. A dedicated expert."* and the brand personality: calm, precise, anti-BS, numbers-first. Every stylistic choice (transition, sound effect, emoji in captions) is filtered through "would a sharp founder post this?"
- **Consistency as a skill** — same caption style, same grade, same intro rhythm across every video. Builds and maintains **templates** so consistency is automatic, not remembered.

---

## 5. Color & Finishing 🟡 WORKING

- **Color correction** — fixes phone footage: white balance, exposure, contrast, accurate skin tones across different shoot days and lighting.
- **Signature grade** — a light, consistent look (slightly warm, clean contrast) applied via saved LUT/preset so all content matches.
- **Export discipline:**
  | Destination | Spec |
  |---|---|
  | IG / LinkedIn Reels | 1080×1920, MP4 (H.264), 30fps, ~10–15 Mbps, AAC audio |
  | LinkedIn feed / YouTube | 1080p or 4K 16:9, H.264/H.265 |
  | Never | Re-exporting an already-compressed export (generation loss), watermarked outputs |

---

## 6. Motion Graphics & Text 🟡 WORKING

- Animated text-ins/outs (clean, fast, 2–4 frame ease — not bouncy PowerPoint).
- Lower thirds for names/titles; logo sting for video ends.
- **Stat callouts** — our content is numbers-heavy (`₹25L → ₹40L`, `+46%`, `7 days`). The editor can make a number land visually: counter animations, scale emphasis, saffron highlight.
- Template building — converts recurring elements (caption preset, hook title, CTA end-card) into reusable templates that cut future edit time.

---

## 7. Tooling 🟡 WORKING — *flexible on which, firm on fluency*

- **One primary NLE mastered** (any one): Premiere Pro · DaVinci Resolve · Final Cut · CapCut (pro-level use).
- **Web/AI stack fluency** — uses AI to go faster, never as a crutch:
  - Auto-captioning (then manual Hinglish proofing)
  - Transcript-based editing (Descript-style word deletion)
  - Silence/filler auto-removal (Kapwing-style)
  - AI denoise / upscale for weak footage
- **Adjacent tools:** Canva/Photoshop basics for thumbnails and cover frames; Google Drive discipline (our structure below).
- **India-team note:** CapCut is banned in India — editors there use VN, DaVinci, Premiere, or web tools (Clipchamp, Kapwing).

## 8. Platform Intelligence 🔴 MUST

Knows that each platform is a different room with different rules:

| Platform | What changes |
|---|---|
| **Instagram Reels** | Fast pacing, trend-aware, hook in 1–3s, karaoke captions, hashtags in caption, audio trends matter |
| **LinkedIn video** | Quieter pacing OK, value-first, 90% watched on mute → captions carry everything, no trending-audio gimmicks, native upload (never a link out) |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Loop-friendly endings, title overlay matters, slightly longer holds acceptable |

One master edit, then **platform-specific exports** — not one file blasted everywhere.

## 9. Workflow & Professionalism 🔴 MUST — *what makes them "embedded", not freelance*

- **Works from the content queue** — picks up the day's script (`queue/YYYY-MM-DD-AM.md` format), checks received footage against the script's beats, **flags gaps before editing**, not after delivery.
- **File hygiene:**
  - Naming: `YYYY-MM-DD-AM_v1.mp4` → matches our pipeline convention
  - Organized project folders (footage / audio / exports / project files)
  - Delivers into the Google Drive structure (`F:\My Drive\AnantaSutra-Content\`)
- **Turnaround discipline** — 24–48h per reel standard; communicates *before* a deadline slips, never after.
- **Revision protocol** — v1 → written feedback → v2 with a change log. Feedback applied to *all future edits*, not just the corrected one. Two revision rounds max as the norm.
- **Ownership** — watches the published post's performance, notices what retained and what dropped, and proposes edit changes based on data. Behaves like the outcome is theirs — because at AnantaSutra, it is.
- **Communication** — reachable in working hours on WhatsApp/Slack, joins the weekly content review, speaks up when a script won't work visually.

## 10. Bonus Skills 🟢

- **Shooting knowledge** — can brief the on-camera person: framing (eyes upper third), lighting a talking head (key + fill from a window), lav vs shotgun audio, phone settings (1080p/30, exposure lock). Massively reduces bad-footage problems upstream.
- **Thumbnail / cover-frame design** — picks and designs the frame that earns the click.
- **Caption copywriting instinct** — tightens a hook line while editing ("Day 63. Still open." beats "It has been 63 days since the role opened").
- **After Effects** — for occasional premium motion work.
- **Basic analytics literacy** — reads retention graphs and knows what a 3-second drop-off means for the next edit.

# 🧪 Vetting Process (fits the AnantaSutra 2–3 profile pipeline)

## The practical test (mandatory — no hire without it)

Send every candidate the **same package**:
1. One raw ~2-minute talking-head clip (real conditions: some ums, a stumble, imperfect lighting)
2. One actual reel script from our queue (hook / beats / on-screen text / CTA)
3. The brand kit (palette, fonts, logo, one reference reel)

**Ask:** deliver one finished 30-second reel in 48 hours, exported for Instagram.

## Scorecard (/10)

| Criterion | Points | What to look for |
|---|---|---|
| Hook lands in 3 seconds | 2 | Did they restructure the raw footage to open strong? |
| Caption accuracy (incl. Hinglish) | 2 | Zero errors = 2. One error = 1. Two+ = 0. |
| Brand adherence | 2 | Palette, font, logo, tone — without being told twice |
| Pacing & cuts | 2 | No dead air, no whiplash; beats match the script |
| Audio quality | 1 | Clean voice, ducked music |
| Delivery discipline | 1 | On time, correct specs, clean filename, no watermark |

**7+ = interview. 9+ = fast-track.**

## Interview questions that expose real editors

1. "Walk me through how you decided the first 3 seconds of your test edit."
2. "The footage doesn't match the script — what do you do?" *(right answer: flag it + propose a re-order/b-roll fix, not silently improvise)*
3. "Same reel for Instagram vs LinkedIn — what changes and why?"
4. "Show me the ugliest feedback you've received and what you changed after it."
5. "What's your template system? Show me one."

# 📈 First-90-Days KPIs (once hired)

| KPI | Target |
|---|---|
| Output | 5 reels/week at brand standard |
| Turnaround | < 48 hours per reel |
| Revision rounds | ≤ 1 average by month 2 |
| Caption error rate | ~0 (spot-checked weekly) |
| Consistency | Any 5 published reels look like one brand when viewed together |
| Initiative | ≥ 2 format/hook improvement proposals per month, based on retention data |

**90-day review question:** *"If this editor left tomorrow, would content quality visibly drop within a week?"* If yes — they've become genuinely embedded. That's the standard.
