### The 30-Second NCR Test
Within 30 seconds the builder checks:
1. ✅ Can you name a local project in his sector?
2. ✅ Do you know one competitor by name?
3. ✅ Can you drop a micro-market slang ("Sector 102 ka traffic", "Yamuna Expressway toll", "RERA-H QPR")?

**Fail any two → line drops. Pass all three → warmed in 30 seconds.**

- **Always accept.** Refusing = insult.
- Builder sends for chai within 30 seconds of you sitting.
- **DO NOT start pitch until chai arrives.** Fill the gap with weather/traffic/cricket.

### Drop-By Culture
- ✅ Works: Udyog Vihar, Noida Sec 62, Faridabad NH-2, Sohna site offices
- ❌ Doesn't work: Cyber Hub / DLF towers (security blocks)
- **Protocol:** WhatsApp first → drop by after 30 min → carry mithai box from Bikanervala/Haldiram (Monday or festival)

### Morning vs Evening
- **Morning (10 AM-1 PM):** Serious business talk — pitch here
- **Evening (post-5 PM):** Relationship talk — family, golf, kids' school, cricket
- **Strategy:** Pitch in morning, bond in evening. Never mix.
