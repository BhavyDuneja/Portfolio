# Public Assets

## Adding Your Professional Portrait

To add your professional portrait to the portfolio:

1. **Save your image** as `portrait.jpg` in the `public/images/` folder
2. **Recommended specifications:**
   - Format: JPG or PNG
   - Dimensions: 800x1000px (portrait orientation)
   - File size: Under 2MB for optimal loading
   - Quality: High resolution for crisp display

3. **Image should be:**
   - Professional headshot or waist-up portrait
   - Good lighting and clear background
   - High quality and well-composed
   - Consistent with your professional brand
